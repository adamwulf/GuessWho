The Contacts app forgets. GuessWho doesn’t.

A phone number is easy. The story behind it — the part you actually want when you see a name again — usually lives only in your head. GuessWho keeps that story, by letting every contact point to the people, events, and notes around it.

👥

Who introduced you

Link one contact to another and note the connection. Open anyone and see the people who brought you together.

📅

Where & when you met

Contacts connect to the events you shared, so you can see the meetup, dinner, or conference where your paths first crossed — and who else was there.

📝

What you talked about

Jot a private note on the person or the event. Next time their name comes up, the details come back with it.

Browser extension

Save a LinkedIn profile in one click.

When you’re looking at someone’s profile in Safari, Chrome, or Brave, click the GuessWho extension. It reads the page you’re already viewing — name, title, company, links, and photo — and hands it to the app.

  • You see a clear before-and-after of exactly what will change.
  • Check off the fields you want; leave the rest untouched.
  • Save creates a new contact or updates an existing one.
  • Nothing is captured or saved without your click and your review.

Everything a contact can carry

GuessWho sits on top of the Contacts and Calendar you already use, and adds the layers that make a name memorable.

🔗

Relationships

Link a person to other people, their company, or an event — each with a note on how you know them.

📅

Events

See the events you shared with a contact, when and where they happened, and who else attended.

📝

Private notes

Keep notes on any person or event. They’re yours alone, right where you’ll look for them.

🏷

Tags

Tag your events to group and find them fast — by trip, project, or occasion.

⚙

Custom fields

Add fields of your own for the details that matter to you and no one else.

👥

Groups

Organizations

Your data stays on your device.

GuessWho reads the Contacts and Calendar already on your device, and stores your notes, tags, links, and favorites as plain files that sync only through your own iCloud. There’s no account to create and no company server — nobody but you can see any of it.
