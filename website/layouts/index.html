Browser extension

Save a LinkedIn profile in one click.

When you’re looking at someone’s profile in Safari, Chrome, or Brave, the GuessWho extension reads the page you’re already viewing — name, title, company, links, and photo — and hands it to the app.

  • Check off exactly which fields to save; leave the rest untouched.
  • Creates a new contact or updates one you already have.
  • Nothing is captured or saved without your click and your review.
