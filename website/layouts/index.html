A phone number is easy. Remembering the person behind it is harder. GuessWho turns a flat contact list into a connected view of the people and places in your life.

Events

Connect contacts to the meetup, dinner, or conference where you met, then see everyone who was there together.

Dated notes

Pick up every conversation where you left off.

Add a private, dated note after each conversation. Open a contact later and you’ll see what you discussed, who connects you, and the events you shared — all on one screen.

  • Dated notes that read like a running memory.
  • Linked people — who introduced you, who they work with.
  • Linked events — where and when your paths crossed.

Browser extension

Save a LinkedIn profile in one click.

When you’re looking at someone’s profile in Safari, Chrome, or Brave, the GuessWho extension reads the page you’re already viewing — name, title, company, links, and photo — and hands it to the app.

  • Check off exactly which fields to save; leave the rest untouched.
  • Creates a new contact or updates one you already have.
  • Nothing is captured or saved without your click and your review.
